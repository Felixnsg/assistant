Re: Change Makers Mentor Position

Dear Makaelah,

I'm writing to apply for the Change Makers Mentor position. The idea that every Change Maker scholar receives a four-year scholarship to Seattle University is incredible. But what really gets me is the program's focus on helping students see themselves as civically engaged leaders. That's not just about college access. It's about changing how young people see their own power.

I understand what it's like to navigate the transition from high school to college without a roadmap. My family immigrated here, and while they wanted the best for me, they didn't know how American higher education worked. The application process, financial aid, choosing a major, even small things like office hours. Everything was a mystery. I figured it out, but I remember wishing someone had been there to guide me. Someone who'd been through it and could say, "Here's what actually matters."

At Bellevue College, I spent two years mentoring students facing similar challenges. Many were first-generation college students. Some were recent immigrants. Others just needed someone to believe in them when they couldn't believe in themselves. I learned that good mentoring isn't about having all the answers. It's about showing up consistently, listening without judgment, and helping students discover their own strengths.

What excites me about Change Makers is the comprehensive approach. Academic support matters, but so does social-emotional readiness. High school students aren't just preparing for college. They're figuring out who they are, what they care about, what kind of adults they want to become. Having mentors who understand that bigger picture, who can support both the homework and the life questions, that makes all the difference.

I'm also drawn to the program's commitment to anti-racist practices and educational justice. As someone who speaks multiple languages and has lived between cultures, I understand how schools can unintentionally create barriers for certain students. Change Makers actively works to remove those barriers. That alignment with my values matters to me.

I can commit to the full academic year, including Saturday sessions and Tuesday/Thursday tutoring at Garfield. I'm ready to co-create curriculum with the grad assistant, bring high energy to every session, and maintain the documentation and communication the program requires. Most importantly, I'm ready to build authentic relationships with scholars and support them through this critical transition.

Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to contributing to Change Makers' mission of empowering Garfield students to graduate with clear paths to their futures.
