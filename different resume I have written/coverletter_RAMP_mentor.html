Re: RAMP Mentor Position at Washington Middle School

Dear Kendall,

I'm writing to apply for the RAMP Mentor position at Washington Middle School. As a Seattle University student who has experienced firsthand the challenges of navigating education as someone from an immigrant family, I understand the importance of having a consistent, caring mentor during those crucial middle school years.

For two years at Bellevue College, I worked as a Peer Educator. I mentored students one-on-one, many of whom reminded me of myself at that age. Confused about academics. Unsure where they belonged. Sometimes just needing someone who would listen without judgment. I learned that showing up consistently matters more than having all the answers. Being there every week, remembering their stories, celebrating their small wins. That's what builds trust.

My experience spans working with students from various backgrounds. I speak multiple languages fluently, including French, Swahili, and several African dialects. But more than language, I understand what it's like to feel caught between cultures. To have parents who want the best for you but don't always understand the American school system. These shared experiences help me connect with students who might otherwise feel isolated.

What draws me to RAMP is your focus on belonging and connection. Middle school is tough. Kids are figuring out who they are while dealing with academic pressure and social dynamics. Having someone in their corner who genuinely cares can change everything. I remember the adults who made that difference for me. They didn't fix all my problems. They just showed up, listened, and helped me believe I could succeed.

I'm particularly interested in your healing-centered approach and commitment to anti-racist practices. Education isn't just about grades. It's about helping young people see their worth and potential despite the barriers they face. I want to be part of creating those transformative relationships with Washington Middle School students.

I can commit to the full academic year, including the November training and professional development. I'm available for the 10 hours per week, understanding that consistency and reliability are essential for building trust with the scholars. I'm ready to work with your team, maintain detailed records, and engage fully in the community you're building.

Thank you for considering my application. I'm excited about the possibility of supporting Washington Middle School students through RAMP and contributing to Seattle University's vision of community engagement.
